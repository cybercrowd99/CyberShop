# CyberCrowd

CyberCrowd is a platform built around people, needs, and trusted exchange.

It connects identity, capability, requests, services, commerce, and records into one organized system.

## What is CyberCrowd?

CyberCrowd helps people discover what they need, find who can help, and create trusted exchanges.

The platform is built around five foundations:

- **Identity** — who is participating
- **Needs** — what is requested
- **Capability** — what can be provided
- **Commerce** — how exchange happens
- **Records** — what is verified and preserved

## Why does it exist?

Many systems are built around transactions first.

CyberCrowd starts with the relationship:

**Who is here, what do they need, and what can they contribute?**

## Core Areas

- **ICAN** — identity and capability
- **NEEDS** — requests and discovery
- **CyberShop** — exchange and marketplace
- **CategorySpine** — organization and classification
- **TransactionSpine** — value movement
- **Ledger** — records and history

## Status

CyberCrowd is under active development.

The goal is to build a trusted foundation for real-world participation and exchange.
